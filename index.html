Formula 1 — Quick Explanation

x + (x + 1) = (x + 1)² − x²

Expanding the right side gives:

(x + 1)² − x² = x² + 2x + 1 − x² = 2x + 1

The left side is also:

x + (x + 1) = 2x + 1

Therefore, both sides are equal.
